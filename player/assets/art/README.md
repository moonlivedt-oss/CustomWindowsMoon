# PlayerMoon artwork

`moon-session.png` was generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for PlayerMoon.
Used as a home banner and as a fallback for tracks without embedded artwork.
Music files and embedded covers are never modified.

Final prompt:

Create a premium atmospheric background artwork for a desktop music player called PlayerMoon. Landscape 3:2 image, no text, no typography, no logo, no UI. Cinematic still life: sculptural smoked glass crescent moon and one polished dark chrome vinyl record floating just above a softly rippling obsidian surface, on the RIGHT THIRD of frame. Delicate warm crimson and dusty rose rim light, subtle deep plum mist, midnight charcoal background. Tasteful tactile realistic 3D photography, soft film grain, rich material detail, contemporary editorial album cover, restrained and elegant, no neon cyberpunk. LEFT TWO THIRDS must remain very dark empty negative space for a title and buttons. Composition must work cropped into a very wide 3:1 horizontal banner. Objects centered vertically in right half so they survive cropping. Palette #101117 #1b1723 #ed647b. No stars or decorative line icons.
